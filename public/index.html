How a release works

Four steps between a budget and a paid contractor.

01 / 04

  1. 01

    Fund the workspace

    Each workspace gets its own Circle developer-controlled wallet on Arc. You send the project budget there in USDC. No shared balances between clients.

    wallet 0xb3cc…ba17 balance USDC
  2. 02

    Park what you don't need yet

    The part of the budget that isn't due soon goes into an Earn Kit USDC vault. It stays there while the work happens and is withdrawn only for the exact amount a release needs.

    vault Earn Kit · USDC withdraw on release
  3. 03

    Approve the work

    When a milestone is delivered, you write down what you checked. That note is stored with the payment, so anyone reviewing the books later sees why it was paid.

    status in progress → approved
  4. 04

    Screen, then pay

    iPay asks CompliR about the contractor's wallet and checks the signed answer. A low verdict pays out on Arc straight away. Anything else stops and tells you why.

    verdict LOW tx confirmed on Arc

The policy

Three verdicts. Each one does exactly one thing.

VerdictWhat happensWhat you do
Low USDC is drawn from the vault if needed and sent to the contractor on Arc. The receipt is checked against the verdict's validity window. Nothing.
Medium The milestone is held with status needs review. No money moves. Look at the reasons and decide.
High The release is blocked and the reason codes are written to the audit trail. Find another way to pay, or don't.

Every release record keeps

  • the milestone, amount and recipient
  • your approval notes
  • the CompliR verdict, reasons and signature
  • the vault withdrawal and payment transactions
  • the yield earned at the moment of release

What it's built on

Off-the-shelf rails, wired together carefully.

Arc

Settlement chain. USDC is the native gas token, so a payment needs nothing else in the wallet.

Circle Wallets

One developer-controlled wallet per workspace, created when you set the workspace up.

Earn Kit

Holds idle USDC in a vault and returns it on withdrawal, with yield tracked per position.

CompliR

Screens the recipient and returns a signed EIP-712 verdict that iPay verifies before sending.
