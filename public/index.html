The Release Pipeline

How iPay executes payment on proof.

A continuous loop from budget deposit to contractor settlement with zero manual escrow friction.

STEP 01

Fund Once

Deposit project budget into your business's dedicated Circle Agent Wallet on Arc Testnet with isolated owner credentials.

STEP 02

Keep It Earning

Idle USDC rests inside the Earn Kit vault, generating continuous yield while contractors work on milestones.

STEP 03

Verify Deliverable

Review work, attach completion evidence, and trigger the pre-flight cryptographic CompliR screening pipeline.

STEP 04

Instant Settlement

iPay pulls only the exact required amount from the Earn vault and settles natively in USDC on Arc with an immutable receipt.

Cryptographic Integrity

Every transaction is backed by signed evidence.

No blind transfers. Prior to authorizing the Arc USDC transaction, the backend requests and cryptographically verifies an EIP-712 signed verdict from CompliR.
