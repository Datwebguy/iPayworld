How a release works

Four steps between a budget and a paid contractor.

01 / 04

  1. 01

    Fund the workspace

    Your workspace gets its own payments account. You add the project budget to it. Nobody else's money is ever mixed with yours.

    account yours alone
  2. 02

    Park what you don't need yet

    The part of the budget that isn't due soon moves into savings and earns interest while the work happens. When a payment needs it, iPay moves back exactly the amount required.

    savings earning moves back when needed
  3. 03

    Approve the work

    When a milestone is delivered, you write down what you checked. That note is stored with the payment, so anyone reviewing the books later sees why it was paid.

    status in progress → approved
  4. 04

    Check, then pay

    iPay runs a risk check on the contractor before any money moves. Low risk is paid straight away. Anything else stops and tells you why.

    risk low payment confirmed

The policy

Three risk results. Each one does exactly one thing.

RiskWhat happensWhat you do
Low The contractor is paid right away, using savings if needed. iPay confirms the payment arrived and keeps the receipt. Nothing.
Medium The milestone is held with status needs review. No money moves. Look at the reasons and decide.
High The payment is blocked and the reason is saved to your history. Find another way to pay, or don't.

Every payment record keeps

  • the milestone, amount and recipient
  • your approval notes
  • the risk result and the reasons for it
  • the payment receipt
  • the interest earned up to that point
