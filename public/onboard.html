Step 1

Name your workspace

Each workspace gets its own wallet, its own milestones and its own audit trail. Nothing is shared with other workspaces.

Step 2

Save the owner key for your workspace

This key unlocks the treasury and approves payments. iPay only stores a hash of it, so if you lose it, nobody can show it to you again.

Step 3

Create the workspace wallet

iPay asks Circle for a new developer-controlled wallet on Arc Testnet. Budget goes in, contractor payments go out from it.

Circle walletNot created yet

Step 4

Fund it with testnet USDC

Request USDC from the Circle faucet so you have something to pay with. On Arc, USDC also pays for gas. You can also send USDC to the address yourself.
